Our Purpose

Knowledge should be accessible, useful, and open to all.

Business Academy A1 is an independent educational initiative designed to make business knowledge easier to explore and understand.

“Better business thinking begins with curiosity, clear questions, and the willingness to keep learning.”

We bring together essential concepts, practical perspectives, and learning resources across business disciplines. Our aim is to encourage thoughtful learning rather than simply memorising terminology.

The Learning Approach

A foundation for better business understanding.

Explore business through connected ideas, thoughtful analysis, and practical questions.

Programme 01

Strategic Management

Understand how organisations analyse their environment, establish direction, and make decisions that shape their future.

About this programme

Strategic management examines the choices organisations make to achieve long-term objectives. It connects external analysis, internal capabilities, competitive positioning, and implementation.

Key topics

  • Introduction to strategic management
  • Vision, mission, and organisational objectives
  • External environment and industry analysis
  • Internal resources and capabilities
  • Competitive advantage and positioning
  • Corporate and business-level strategy
  • Strategy implementation and evaluation

Questions to consider

  • Why do some organisations maintain a competitive advantage?
  • How should a business respond to changes in its environment?
  • What makes a strategy effective in practice?

Suggested starting points

Explore introductory material on strategy, competitive advantage, and organisational analysis.

Programme 02

Organisational Behaviour

Explore how individuals and groups behave within organisations, and how leadership and culture influence performance.

About this programme

Organisational behaviour studies the human side of organisations. It examines individual behaviour, group dynamics, leadership, motivation, communication, and organisational culture.

Key topics

  • Foundations of organisational behaviour
  • Personality, perception, and individual differences
  • Motivation and job satisfaction
  • Groups, teams, and collaboration
  • Leadership and influence
  • Organisational culture and change
  • Conflict, communication, and negotiation

Questions to consider

  • What motivates people at work?
  • How does organisational culture influence behaviour?
  • What distinguishes effective leadership?

Suggested starting points

Explore research and learning resources on leadership, people management, and organisational behaviour.

Programme 03

Corporate Finance

Discover how organisations make financial decisions, evaluate investments, manage risk, and allocate capital.

About this programme

Corporate finance focuses on how businesses raise and use funds. It explores investment decisions, financing choices, valuation, financial risk, and the relationship between financial decisions and organisational value.

Key topics

  • Foundations of corporate finance
  • Financial statements and analysis
  • Time value of money
  • Investment appraisal and capital budgeting
  • Risk and return
  • Capital structure and financing
  • Business valuation and financial planning

Questions to consider

  • How should a business evaluate an investment?
  • What is the relationship between risk and expected return?
  • How do financing decisions affect organisational value?

Suggested starting points

Explore introductory finance resources and materials on financial decision-making.

  • How can marketing create meaningful customer value?

Programme 05

Managerial Economics

Apply economic reasoning to business decisions, market behaviour, competition, and the allocation of resources.

About this programme

Managerial economics uses economic concepts and analytical tools to help decision-makers understand markets, assess alternatives, and make informed business choices.

Key topics

  • Economic principles for managers
  • Demand, supply, and market equilibrium
  • Consumer choice and elasticity
  • Production and cost analysis
  • Market structures and competition
  • Pricing and business decisions
  • Economic uncertainty and decision-making

Questions to consider

  • How does price elasticity influence business decisions?
  • Why do different market structures produce different outcomes?
  • How can economic analysis improve resource allocation?

Suggested starting points

Explore introductory economics materials and resources on markets and decision-making.

Programme 06

Corporate Governance

Examine accountability, ethical leadership, corporate oversight, and responsible organisational decision-making.

About this programme

Corporate governance explores the systems and relationships through which organisations are directed and controlled. It considers the responsibilities of boards, executives, shareholders, and other stakeholders.

Key topics

  • Principles of corporate governance
  • Boards of directors and their responsibilities
  • Shareholders and stakeholder interests
  • Accountability and transparency
  • Business ethics and responsible leadership
  • Risk management and internal controls
  • Governance, sustainability, and trust

Questions to consider

  • Who should be accountable for corporate decisions?
  • How can organisations balance stakeholder interests?
  • Why does transparency matter in business?

Suggested starting points

Explore research and resources on governance, accountability, and business ethics.
